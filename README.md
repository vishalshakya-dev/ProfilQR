# Welcome to ProfilQR!

Welcome to ProfilQR, the ultimate platform! It's all about making profile sharing easy peasy.

## How It Works:

This project helps you share your profile easily using QR codes. Here's how it works:

1. Fill in your details like bio, contact info, and more.
2. Generate a QR code using the "Create Profile" button.
3. Share the QR code with your friends and family or stick it on your business card, website, or social media.
4. Anyone who scans your code is directed straight to your profile.

## Why Choose This:

- Simple and easy to use.
- Seamless sharing. Perfect for networking, freelancers, or sharing with friends.
- Your data is only accessible to those who scan your code.
